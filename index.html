🎮

Multi-Agent Game Theory

Studying strategic interactions between large numbers of rational agents using mean-field game frameworks and equilibrium concepts.

Reinforcement Learning

Developing algorithms for agents to learn optimal policies in complex, high-dimensional, partially observable environments.

Control Theory

Bridging data-driven methods with classical control systems, including differential flatness and Gaussian process approaches.

Background

About Me

I am a Machine Learning PhD student at the Georgia Institute of Technology, Atlanta. I work in the Dynamics and Control Systems Laboratory (DCSL) under the guidance of Prof. Panagiotis Tsiotras. My research sits at the intersection of game theory, reinforcement learning, and decision making. I am particularly interested in how large populations of strategic agents can learn to cooperate or compete — and how we can design systems that are safe, risk-averse and robust to uncertainties.

Before Georgia Tech, I earned my undergraduate and master's degrees at IIT Bombay where I worked on control theory and analysis, particularly, learning-based control and geometric mechanics.

When I am not working on mathemtical proofs or training neural networks (hyperparameter tuning yay!), you can find me running around Atlanta, lifting weights, scouting for coffee shops or cooking.
