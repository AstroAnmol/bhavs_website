Game Theory RL

Robust Mean-Field Games Under Bounded Rationality

Developing equilibrium concepts for mean-field games where agents exhibit bounded rationality and risk-averse preferences. The goal is to find robust strategies that remain near-optimal under perturbations in opponent behavior distributions.

Multi-Agent Mean Field

Large-Scale Competitive Team Learning

Studying how teams of agents can learn coordinated competitive strategies against opponents using mean-field game approximations. Incorporates online opponent modeling to adapt in real time to changing adversary strategies.

Theory Equilibrium

Equilibrium Learning in Heterogeneous Populations

Exploring how equilibria emerge when a population of agents with heterogeneous objectives and capabilities interact. Focus on convergence guarantees and the structure of approximate Nash equilibria at scale.

Control Data-Driven

Data-Driven Adaptive Cruise Control

Designed a data-driven controller for adaptive cruise control using differential flatness to convert the problem into a flat output tracking task, and Gaussian processes to model and account for uncertainty in vehicle dynamics.

Published · ACC 2024
Paper →

Control Stability

Switched PD Control for Spherical Pendulum

Designed a switched PD controller achieving almost global asymptotic stability for the spherical pendulum — an underactuated benchmark system. Established theoretical stability guarantees for the switching policy.

Published · MED 2021
