Robust Mean-Field Games with Risk Aversion and Bounded Rationality

Bhavini Jeloka, Yue Guan, Panagiotis Tsiotras

arXiv preprint: 2602.13353

Preprint

Mean-field games address scalability in large-population multi-agent systems by reducing inter-agent interactions to those between a representative agent and an aggregate population distribution. However, standard mean-field problem formulations assume either fully rational agents and/or a fixed and known initial agent distribution. To address these limitations, we introduce a unified framework that incorporates risk aversion to uncertainty in the initial population distribution together with an explicit quantal-response model of bounded rationality, leading to a generalized equilibrium concept, which we call the mean-field risk-averse quantal response equilibrium (MF-RQE). We prove the existence of MF-RQE, establish convergence guarantees for fixed-point iteration and fictitious play, and propose a scalable reinforcement learning algorithm for large state–action spaces. Learning Large-Scale Competitive Team Behaviors with Mean-Field Interactions and Online Opponent Modeling

Bhavini Jeloka, Yue Guan, Panagiotis Tsiotras

ALA (Adaptive and Learning Agents) Workshop, AAMAS · 2025
Also submitted to ICLR 2026

Workshop

We propose a scalable framework for large-scale multi-team competitive games using mean-field approximations coupled with online opponent model estimation. Our approach enables agents to efficiently learn competitive team strategies while adapting to evolving opponent behaviors in real time.

Data-Driven Control of Adaptive Cruise Control Systems Using Differential Flatness and Gaussian Processes

Florentina Nicolau, Adnane Saoud, Bhavini Jeloka, Ravi N. Banavar

2024 American Control Conference (ACC) · July 2024

Conference

We present a data-driven control framework for adaptive cruise control (ACC) systems that leverages differential flatness to simplify trajectory planning and Gaussian processes for uncertainty quantification. Our approach enables safe and efficient vehicle following under uncertain road conditions.

Almost Globally Asymptotically Stable Switched PD Control for a Spherical Pendulum

Bhavini Jeloka et al.

2021 Mediterranean Conference on Control and Automation (MED) · June 2021

Conference

We design and analyze a switched proportional-derivative (PD) controller achieving almost global asymptotic stability for the underactuated spherical pendulum system. The switching logic ensures the system can be driven to the upright equilibrium from almost all initial conditions.
